Tiny Transformer, Step by Step

A complete encoder–decoder Transformer, small enough that every number fits on screen. Follow one sentence forward from tokens to loss, follow the gradient backward to every weight, then train it and watch attention organise itself.

1The toy task: reverse a sequence

The model reads up to four letters and must write them backwards, then stop with </s>. Short inputs are padded, so you will see both kinds of mask at work.

Everything below updates when you change this.

2Forward, backward, update

Step through every operation. Hover any cell to see which cells it was computed from, or which cells it feeds. In the backward pass the same links carry the gradient the other way.

3Train it

Repeat forward → backward → update on many random sequences (batches of 32, Adam optimiser). The loss drops and the attention maps for your input change from noise into structure. Afterwards, drag the slider to any point in training: section 2 then shows the model as it was at that step.
