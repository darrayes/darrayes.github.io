1. Markov Decision Processes & Bellman Equations

The agent–environment loop, the Markov property, dynamics, policies, returns, state and action values, the Bellman expectation equations in scalar and matrix form, and the Bellman optimality equations.

Contractions and max-norm stopping, synchronous and in-place policy evaluation, the policy improvement theorem, policy iteration, value iteration, GPI and a complete worked walkthrough on a delivery-drone MDP.
